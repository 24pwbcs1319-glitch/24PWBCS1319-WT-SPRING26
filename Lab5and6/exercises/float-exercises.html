Float-Based Magazine

Column focus – Floating columns lets text flow in a multi-column layout just like a magazine spread. Use consistent widths (e.g., 48%) and gutters to maintain rhythm.
Column two – The content stays balanced because both pieces float next to each other, and the parent clears them with the helper class.

Images float inside the text block to produce classic wrap-around copy. On desktop the picture anchors the paragraph, and on mobile it centers above the content.

Sidebar

Responsive behavior shifts the sidebar below the article on small screens while still keeping the float-based structure on larger viewports.
